## Scene-by-scene script

Timecodes are a guide for a ~70s cut (~7–8s per scene).

### 1 · Hook — 0:00–0:07
- **On-screen:** "Bonds, explained in 60 seconds" · *Fixed income, simplified.*
- **Voiceover:** "Your savings account earns a few percent. Bonds are
  how millions of investors aim to do better — here's how they work."

### 2 · What is a bond — 0:07–0:16
- **On-screen:** "A bond = a loan you give." / "You lend → they pay
  fixed interest → you get your money back."
- **VO:** "A bond is simply a loan. You lend money to a company or the
  government. In return, they pay you fixed interest, then return your
  principal on a set date."

### 3 · Why investors like bonds — 0:16–0:25
- **On-screen:** "Predictable income." / "You know your return upfront —
  unlike stocks."
- **Visual:** A steady flat line vs a jagged stock line.
- **VO:** "Unlike stocks, a bond tells you your return upfront. That
  predictability is why bonds anchor so many portfolios."

### 4 · Key term: YTM — 0:25–0:34
- **On-screen:** "Meet YTM — Yield to Maturity" / "Your total annual
  return if you hold till maturity."
- **VO:** "The number to know is Y-T-M — yield to maturity. It's your
  total annual return if you hold the bond until it matures."

### 5 · Types of bonds in India — 0:34–0:44
- **On-screen (tiers):** "Government — safest" / "Bank bonds" / "NBFC
  bonds & NCDs — higher yield"
- **VO:** "In India you'll see government bonds — the safest — then bank
  bonds, and NBFC bonds and NCDs, which typically offer higher yields."

### 6 · Yield vs risk — 0:44–0:53
- **On-screen:** "Higher yield = higher risk." / "Credit ratings — AAA,
  AA — show how safe the issuer is."
- **Visual:** Shield with AAA / AA badges.
- **VO:** "But higher yield means higher risk. Credit ratings — triple-A,
  double-A — are your quick read on how safe an issuer is."

### 7 · The opportunity — 0:53–1:02
- **On-screen:** "Did you know?" / "Quality NBFC bonds & NCDs can offer
  3–4% more yield than AAA." (+300–450 bps)
- **Visual:** Short AAA bar vs taller NBFC-yield bar.
- **VO:** "Here's what many miss: quality NBFC bonds and NCDs can pay
  three to four percent more than top-rated bonds — meaningful extra
  income for the right investor."

### 8 · Access — 1:02–1:10
- **On-screen:** "Start with as little as ₹10,000." / "Buy & sell bonds
  online."
- **VO:** "And you no longer need lakhs to begin — you can start
  investing in bonds online with as little as ten thousand rupees."

### 9 · Subtle CTA / Outro — 1:10–1:18
- **On-screen:** "Learn the bond market. Invest with confidence." /
  *MeraDhan.co — Fixed income, simplified.* / small: "Educational only.
  Not investment advice."
- **VO:** "Learn the bond market and invest with confidence. MeraDhan —
  fixed income, simplified."
